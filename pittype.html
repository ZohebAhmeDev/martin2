Pit Type RCC Weighbridges

Flush ground-level reinforced concrete weighbridges engineered for space efficiency and multi-directional traffic flow.

Reinforced Concrete Series

Pit Type RCC Weighbridges

This type of weighbridge, also known as a truck scale, is designed to be installed in a pit. It is ideal for locations with limited space, particularly in non-hilly areas where the construction of a pit is not prohibitively expensive. The platform is level with the ground, allowing vehicles to approach the weighbridge from any direction.

The platform is constructed from concrete, ensuring a long lifespan and minimal maintenance requirements. Additionally, the platform is cast on-site, ensuring a perfect fit and maximum accuracy.

Overall, the Pit Type RCC Weighbridge is an excellent choice for businesses looking to accurately weigh vehicles in a space-efficient and cost-effective manner.

Benefits:
  • Economical
  • Maintenance free Platform
  • No painting & Re-painting required.
  • Stronger, heavier and more stable
  • Longer Life
  • Higher Safety
